Privacy

UTAG is a public, read-only catalog of music metadata. It has no accounts, no sign-up, and no tracking.

The site reads artist, release, track, and artwork records from a public database and displays them in your browser. Reading the catalog sends no personal information anywhere: there are no analytics, no cookies set by this site, and no advertising.

Artwork images load from the public sources credited on each record. Your browser may contact those image hosts directly, the same as loading any image on the web.

Questions about a record, or a correction to one, belong in the review process described on the ledger. Last updated October 2026.
