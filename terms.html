Terms

UTAG is a reference catalog of music metadata: artist names, release titles, editions, dates, labels, track listings, and artwork, each checked against independent sources before it is marked verified.

Records carry a confidence state. A record marked verified has been cross-checked; a record marked needs review, conflicting, or unknown has not finished that process and may change. The catalog is provided as is, for reference.

Album artwork belongs to its respective rights holders and is shown at small size for identification, credited to its source on each record. Metadata facts themselves (titles, dates, track listings) are factual data.

If you hold rights to artwork shown here and want it removed, or you spot a wrong record, say so and it will be reviewed. Last updated October 2026.
